Frequently asked questions

What is the Academy?

“My Academy” is your personal school inside Melaymade: courses, lessons with materials from your stock, students and course income. All next to your cost calculation.

Can students learn right in the app?

Not yet: for now you manage students and their progress yourself. A student account and a shared course chat are in development.

Can I upload lessons to platforms?

It is planned: uploading lessons to handmade platforms in one tap. It is in development and we are not giving exact dates yet.

Do I need to install it from an app store?

No. Open the app in your browser and add it to your home screen. It works like a regular app.

Is my data safe?

Every user has their own separate stock that nobody else can see. Data is stored in a secure cloud in Europe (Paris).

Does it work offline?

Yes. Changes are saved on your device and sent to the cloud when you are back online.

Which languages are supported?

Russian for now. Turkish and English are on the way.

How much does it cost?

A trial first, then a subscription. We will announce pricing soon.

What happens when my subscription ends?

Your data stays safe and you can save a copy of it at any time.
