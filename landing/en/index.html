Still wondering?

Will it work for my kind of craft?

Yes, if your piece is built from materials you can count: beads, stones, yarn, fabric, clay, hair, wax and more. Not sure? Try it free with your own materials.

What is the Academy?

“My Academy” is your personal school inside My Workshop: courses, lessons with materials from your stock, students and course income. All next to your cost calculation.

Can students learn right in the app?

Not yet: for now you manage students and their progress yourself. A student account and a shared course chat are in development.

Can I upload lessons to platforms?

It is planned: uploading lessons to handmade platforms in one tap. It is in development and we are not giving exact dates yet.

Do I need to install it from an app store?

No. Open the app in your browser and add it to your home screen. It works like a regular app.

Is my data safe?

Every user has their own separate stock that nobody else can see. Data is stored in a secure cloud in Europe (Paris).

Does it work offline?

Yes. Changes are saved on your device and sent to the cloud when you are back online.

Which languages are supported?

Russian, Turkish and English. You pick the language on the login screen and in the “More” section.

How much does it cost?

14 days free after you sign up, no card needed. Then ₺599 per month or ₺5,750 per year (20% off). Prices may change before payments open.

How does the trial work?

The 14 days start when you register and every feature is available. When the trial ends your data stays, and you need a subscription to keep working.

What happens when my subscription ends?

Your data stays safe and you can save a copy of it at any time.
